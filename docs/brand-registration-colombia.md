# Dog-Food Brand & Product Registration in Colombia

Research notes on the steps required to register a dog-food brand and its products in Colombia. Two authorities are involved: the SIC for trademark/brand registration and INVIMA for product sanitary registration.

## SIC — Trademark / Brand Registration

The Superintendencia de Industria y Comercio (SIC) handles brand and trademark registration in Colombia. Registration is carried out through the SIPI platform.

- Authority: Superintendencia de Industria y Comercio (SIC)
- Scope: Brand / trademark registration
- Platform: SIPI

## INVIMA — Product Sanitary Registration

The Instituto Nacional de Vigilancia de Medicamentos y Alimentos (INVIMA) handles pet-food product registration. The process follows three steps:

1. Identify the product's risk category.
2. Register the factory / establishment.
3. Obtain the sanitary registration, permit, or notification.
